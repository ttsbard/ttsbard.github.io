Text-to-speech for Windows

Turn text into speech, quickly

Open the editor with a hotkey, type a phrase and send it. TTSBard brings speech, on-screen text and sound reactions together for everyday communication, gaming and streaming.

Windows 10/11 · Open source · English and Russian interface

  1. Open the editor

    Default shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+F3. The window opens above other applications, ready to type.

  2. Type and send

    Write your phrase and press Enter to speak it.

  3. Keep the conversation going

    Keep typing, hide the window or return focus to your previous application, depending on your settings.

Voices and audio

Choose your voice. Make it sound right.

Use local voice models or connect a cloud service, then adjust pitch, tempo and volume in one application.

Local and cloud voices

Piper runs on your computer after you install a voice model. Silero Bot works through your Telegram account. OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Fish Audio and compatible local HTTP servers are also supported.

Cloud services may require an account, API key or payment.

Automatic audio cache

TTSBard caches synthesized audio and automatically reuses it when you send the same text with the same synthesis settings, without another request to the TTS service or additional quota usage.

Hear yourself and reach your friends

Send speech to headphones or speakers and a virtual audio cable at the same time. Set their volume independently.

A virtual audio cable must be installed and configured separately for games and voice chats.

Before you download

Is TTSBard free?

TTSBard is open-source software under GPL-3.0-only. External TTS services have their own access requirements, quotas and pricing.

Can I use it without the internet?

Piper synthesizes speech locally after you install a compatible voice model. Cloud voices and online integrations require a network connection.

Can people hear it in a game or Discord?

Yes, when you route TTSBard through a configured virtual audio cable and select that cable as the microphone in the game or voice application.

Which systems and languages are supported?

TTSBard supports Windows 10 and 11. The interface is available in English and Russian; speech languages depend on the provider and voice model.
